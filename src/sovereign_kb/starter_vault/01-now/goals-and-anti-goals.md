# Goals, Strategic Theses & Anti-Goals

## 18–24 Month North Star Leverage Model
> **Primary Objective**: Focus execution on compounding product equity, landmark scientific research, and autonomous tooling while eliminating fragmented context switching.

---

## Active Strategic Pillars
1. **Scale a Dominant Flagship Product**: Build and automate recurring product value ([[03-life-domains/ventures-and-engineering]]).
2. **Autonomous Sovereign Compute & Agentic Leverage**: Delegate repetitive verification, testing, and scaffolding to deterministic agents ([[02-operating-system/autonomous-engineering-framework]]).
3. **Landmark Research & Authorship**: Ship falsifiable, reproducible scientific work ([[02-operating-system/scientific-paper-framework]]).

---

## Explicit Anti-Goals (What I Refuse to Optimize For)
- **No Administrative Drag or Bureaucratic Fragmentation**: Keep schedule utilization $\rho$ well below the congestion cliff.
- **No Cloud Lock-In or Rented Fragility**: Own the stack and data in local-first formats.
- **No Unleveraged Bespoke Trap**: Prioritize work that compounds into reusable IP, frameworks, or recurring products.
