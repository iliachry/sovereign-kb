# Philosophy & Worldview

## 1. Error as Information, Not Identity
Complex minds and resilient engineering systems do not emerge from sterile perfection; they emerge from bounded error correction, falsifiable hypotheses, and closed-loop feedback.

## 2. Sovereign Ownership Over Rented Fragility
Own your knowledge graph in plain-text Markdown, index it locally in SQLite, embed it with local weights, and verify every software or scientific artifact through deterministic offline gates.

## 3. Non-Linear Quality Barriers
As formalized in [[02-operating-system/heuristics-and-mental-models]], system health is governed by multiplicative barrier gates rather than linear averages: a single catastrophic failure in foundational health, security, or mathematical soundness zeroes out surface-level velocity.
